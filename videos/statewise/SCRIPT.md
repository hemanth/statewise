# Statewise narration

**Voice:** Hemanth, Pocket TTS local safetensors voice profile
**Voice direction:** Direct, warm, conversational.

## Line 1 — discover (Frame 1)

    Fifty states. Where do you start? Pick one. Statewise turns a map of the United States into a little discovery, every time you explore.

## Line 2 — learn (Frame 2)

    Tap California. There is the capital, the year it became a state, and a fact worth remembering. Mark it as learned, and keep exploring.

## Line 3 — practice (Frame 3)

    Now try the map challenge. Pick the wrong state, and you see exactly where you went wrong. The correct outline lights up. Try again, and make it stick.

## Line 4 — remember (Frame 4)

    Practice capitals and nicknames too. Your progress stays on your device, and the map works on your phone. Open Statewise. Learn one state today.
